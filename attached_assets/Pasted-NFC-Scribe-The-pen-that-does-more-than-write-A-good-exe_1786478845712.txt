NFC Scribe
The pen that does more than write.
A good executive pen already has a job to do.
The NFC Scribe just gives it another one.
At first glance, it looks like a polished metal pen with a soft-touch finish and modern gunmetal trim. But built inside is NFC technology that allows someone to tap the pen with a smartphone or tablet and instantly connect to a programmed website.
No typing. No searching. No QR code to scan.
Just tap and go.
Why it’s trending
Promotional products are getting smarter, and the best ones are combining something familiar with technology that makes them more useful.
That is exactly what makes the NFC Scribe interesting.
A pen is still one of the most practical branded products you can give someone. Add NFC technology, and suddenly that same pen can become a direct connection to your website, digital business card, event page, product information, recruiting page or sales presentation.
It gives the recipient a reason to interact with the product instead of simply noticing the logo.
One tap can lead almost anywhere
The NFC chip can be programmed to connect users directly to a website, which gives you plenty of ways to build it into a campaign.
Use it to link to:
•	A digital business card
•	Your company website
•	A sales rep landing page
•	A recruiting or careers page
•	An event registration page
•	A product launch
•	A downloadable brochure or catalog
•	A customer portal
•	A conference agenda
•	A personalized thank-you page
The pen stays physical. The experience keeps going online.
Made to feel like a better pen
The technology is the fun part, but this still needs to be something people want to write with.
The oval-shaped barrel is designed for a comfortable grip and finished with a soft-touch coating. Choose from deep jewel tones or matte black, paired with gunmetal trim for a more elevated look.
There is also plenty of room for branding, including laser engraving or optional full-color imprinting.
That makes it a strong choice when you want something more polished than a standard promotional pen without moving into an overly expensive executive gift.
Optional NFC locking
For programs where control matters, optional NFC locking is available.
Each pen can be individually programmed and then locked so the NFC destination cannot be reprogrammed later.

•	Recruiting campaigns

•	Distributed employee programs
